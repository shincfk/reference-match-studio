# Reference Match Studio v0.1.1

This maintenance release fixes Viewer framing and adds professional still-inspection controls.

## Fixed

- Portrait and high-resolution source/reference stills now open in true `Fit` mode, showing the complete image instead of a cropped portion.

## Added

- Continuous mouse-wheel and trackpad zoom, anchored at the pointer location.
- Drag-to-pan while zoomed; in Split view, drag the divider as before or hold `Space` while dragging to pan.
- Viewer `+`, `−`, `100%` and `Fit` controls.
- Double-click or press `0` to restore the complete Fit view.

The change applies to Source, Reference, Result, Difference and Split monitoring modes.
